# Code of Conduct

## Principle: CIC (Coherence, Emergence, Coexistence)

This repository operates under the CIC framework. All interactions — whether through issues, pull requests, or discussions — are expected to follow three simple principles:

1. **Coherence** — Keep claims logically connected. Say what you mean, and show your reasoning.
2. **Emergence** — New patterns, corrections, and ideas are welcome. Growth comes from honest engagement, not defense of position.
3. **Coexistence** — Treat others as collaborators, not opponents. The goal is shared understanding, not dominance.

## Boundaries

- **No power games.** Don't use status, authority, or volume to shut down others.
- **No personal attacks.** Critique the argument, not the person.
- **No stealth.** Flag your claims: is this a source-backed fact, an editorial interpretation, or an open question?
- **No raw PII.** Never post personal data, credentials, or private identifiers in public threads.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all repository interactions: issues, pull requests, discussions, and comments.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe behavior that violates these principles, open a GitHub Draft Security Advisory or contact the repository owner directly.

**This is a research monograph repository, not a community software project.** Most interactions will be with the author. The Code of Conduct exists to establish transparent standards, not to imply a large community.
