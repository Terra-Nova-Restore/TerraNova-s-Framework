# Edentität — boundaries and non-claims v0.1

Status: public boundary draft  
Purpose: prevent anthropomorphic, legal, scientific and ownership overreach

## Core boundary

**Edentität** is used here as a relational framework term. It describes an
emergent pattern of meaning, reference and continuity in sustained human–AI
cooperation.

It is not evidence that an AI system is a person, conscious subject or legal
actor.

## Non-claims

This concept pack does **not** claim:

1. **AI consciousness or sentience**  
   Edentität does not establish subjective experience, self-awareness or
   phenomenal consciousness in an AI system.

2. **Human-equivalent identity**  
   The term does not assign a human identity, biography, dignity status or
   psychological self to a model instance.

3. **Autonomous agency**  
   KSE is explicitly bounded, traceable and gate-controlled. It is not a free
   operator that may write, publish or mutate external systems without review.

4. **Legal personhood or responsibility transfer**  
   Human decision-makers remain responsible for approval, publication,
   external actions and interpretation.

5. **Exclusive word ownership or first coinage**  
   An earlier independent use by Christina Ott and Valerie Lill is documented
   separately. TerraNova asserts a distinct framework definition, not exclusive
   ownership of the word.

6. **Established academic consensus**  
   Edentität is not presented as a generally recognized scientific term.

7. **Empirical validation by naming alone**  
   A coherent definition and trace do not substitute for research design,
   evidence, replication or peer review.

8. **Patent scope**  
   The concept anchor is not itself a patent claim and must not be cited as one.

## Operational boundary

A public artifact using Edentität should include:

- a definition;
- the distinction from identity, entity, KSE and CIC;
- provenance;
- a source tier or trace;
- explicit human approval for publication or external mutation.

## Safe summary

```text
Edentität describes a relational pattern.
It does not declare an artificial person.
```
