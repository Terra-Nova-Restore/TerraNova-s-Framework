## Why provenance matters

The word **Edentität** has more than one documented line of use. The public
record must therefore separate **word history**, **meaning** and **framework
integration** instead of implying exclusive authorship.

## External use: Ott / Lill

TerraNova source material records an external use by **Christina Ott and
Valerie Lill** in the chapter:

> *Edentität – Vom Ursprung der Identität*

in the book *Was lange gärt, wird endlich Mut* (reported in the internal source
material as Francke Verlag, 2023).

The external line is described as language-play around origin, identity,
biographical meaning and the association **Eden + Identität**.

Direct correspondence in June 2026 confirmed that Valerie Lill coined the word
through playful rearrangement of language and that quotation was permitted.
The raw correspondence is retained outside this public repository.

## TerraNova use

The TerraNova / FerrAI line uses **Edentität** differently:

- as an emergent and relational meaning/reference form;
- as something that stabilizes between human, system, language and context;
- as a foundation term connected to the Semantic Core Layer and CIC;
- with **KSE — Kooperative Semantische Edentität** as the bounded,
  addressable and gate-controlled system form.

## Separation rule

```text
Ott / Lill use    != TerraNova use
same word         != same definition
external evidence != exclusive ownership
```

The two lines may be compared, but they must not be merged or presented as one
continuous authorship claim.

## Claims intentionally not made

This repository does not claim:

- first or exclusive coinage of the word;
- ownership of the Ott / Lill meaning;
- that Edentität is an established academic term;
- that the concept has already been scientifically validated;
- that the word itself is a patent claim or protected product name.
