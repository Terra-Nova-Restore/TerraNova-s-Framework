## Public Boundary

- DOI and version lineage
- CAP concept and control-layer explanation
- aggregate registry status

- no-credit operating policy
- release-readiness gates

Blocked:

- raw Notion URLs or IDs beyond public/control anchors
- raw private page lists
- restricted wiki child titles
- private chat/export remnants
- token, wallet, credential, account or secret material
- patent/IP details beyond already public-safe metadata
